What I Do & How I Can Help.

RNA-seq & scRNA-seq

End-to-end transcriptomics analysis — from raw reads to biological insight. I work with both bulk and single-cell datasets, performing differential expression, pathway enrichment, and cell-type annotation.

Variant Discovery (WES/WGS)

GATK best-practice whole-exome and whole-genome sequencing pipelines. During my ICCBS fellowship I identified 884 high-impact variants in Acute Myeloid Leukemia samples.

ML for Biomarker Discovery

Applying Random Forest, XGBoost, and LASSO regression to genomic data for feature selection and predictive modelling — bridging statistics and biological meaning.

GWAS & Population Genomics

Genotype-phenotype association studies with downstream LD analysis and Manhattan plot generation. I enjoy making sense of complex population-level genetic data.

Systematic Reviews & Meta-Analysis

Rigorous evidence synthesis using PRISMA methodology. I've contributed to multi-study syntheses that establish effect-size baselines for genomic research.

Wet Lab & Experimental Biology

Hands-on experience in tissue culture, PCR/qPCR, DNA extraction, gel electrophoresis, biochemical assays, and microbial isolation — I'm as comfortable at the bench as at the terminal.
